# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project Name
Digital Lending Credit Risk Analysis

## Business Objective

To develop an analytics solution that helps the lending team identify risky customers and reduce loan defaults.

---

# Business Goals

1. Reduce credit risk exposure
2. Improve loan approval decisions
3. Identify high-risk customer segments
4. Monitor loan portfolio performance

---

# Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Credit Risk Team | Evaluate borrower risk |
| Business Managers | Monitor portfolio performance |
| Loan Officers | Approve/reject applications |
| Data Analysts | Generate insights |

---

# Business Questions

1. What is the overall default rate?
2. Which employment groups have higher defaults?
3. Does credit score impact repayment?
4. Which loan purposes have maximum risk?
5. Which branches have higher default rates?
