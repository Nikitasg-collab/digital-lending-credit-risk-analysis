# KPI Definitions


## Total Loans

Number of approved loan applications


Formula:

COUNT(Loan_ID)


---

## Total Loan Amount

Total money issued as loans


Formula:

SUM(Loan_Amount)


---

## Default Rate


Formula:

(Default Loans / Total Loans) * 100


Example:

3187 / 10000 *100

=31.87%


---

## Approval Rate


Formula:

Approved Applications / Total Applications *100


---

## Average Loan Amount


Formula:

Total Loan Amount / Total Loans
