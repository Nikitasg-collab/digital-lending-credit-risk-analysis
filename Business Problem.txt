# Business Problem Statement

Digital lending platforms process thousands of loan applications every month.

However, increasing loan defaults impact:
- Revenue
- Cash flow
- Risk exposure
- Customer portfolio quality


The business wants to analyze historical loan data to answer:

1. What factors contribute to loan defaults?
2. Which customer segments are high risk?
3. Which loan products have higher default rates?
4. How can risk teams improve loan approval decisions?


The project focuses on analyzing customer demographics, financial attributes, loan characteristics, and repayment behavior.
